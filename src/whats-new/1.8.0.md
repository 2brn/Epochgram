## Calendar sync

Sync ICS calendars from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, and other compatible services straight into your vault. Each event becomes one note on the timeline, supports recurring rules via `recur`, and can be synced manually with **⌘ Sync calendar** or on a schedule. [Read more](https://www.epochgram.com/docs#calendar-sync-pro).
