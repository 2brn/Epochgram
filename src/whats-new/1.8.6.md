## Quick filter commands

You can now toggle timeline filters directly from the command palette (or assign a hotkey):

- **⌘ Epochgram: Toggle reviewed only**
- **⌘ Epochgram: Toggle tracked changes** (Pro)
- **⌘ Epochgram: Toggle content dates**
- **⌘ Epochgram: Toggle attachments**
- **⌘ Epochgram: Toggle Epochs** (Pro)
