## Pin modes: today, date, dock

You can now pin a note with explicit modes instead of a single on/off state. This makes pinned records easier to place and keep in the view you expect.

![feature](images/1.7.0/pin-modes.webp)

---

## Marks and pins in the note YAML

Pin and mark states now live in note frontmatter, including explicit mark hex colors. This keeps visible state more consistent when files move or are reopened.

---

## AI bridge in the right side-bar

You can now open the AI bridge in an Obsidian webviewer leaf in the right side-bar. This keeps AI status and controls visible without leaving your current workspace.

---

## Other improvements

- Reduced typing-time stutter by deferring heavy reindex and refresh work.
- Reset now excludes separate Marks and Pinned actions to avoid accidental note-content updates.
- Small settings wording and UI consistency fixes.
