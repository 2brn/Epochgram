## Pin modes: today, date, dock

```yaml
pin: today # pinned to the current day (same via menu/cmd).
```

![pin-today](https://www.epochgram.com/images/1.7.0-pin-today.png)

---

```yaml
pin: date # pinned to its date and always visible at any zoom level; 
          # badge supports context menu and drag-and-drop;
          # click opens note, double-click focuses its date.
```

![pin-date](https://www.epochgram.com/images/1.7.0-pin-date.png)

---

```yaml
pin: dock # like date, but sticks to the top or bottom when out of view.
```

![pin-dock](https://www.epochgram.com/images/1.7.0-pin-dock.png)

---

## Marks and pins in the note YAML

Pin and mark states now live in note frontmatter, including explicit mark hex colors. This keeps the visible state more consistent without unnecessarily syncing the index.

```yaml
mark: "#d04255"
```

---

## AI bridge in the right side-bar

You can now open the AI bridge in an Obsidian webviewer leaf in the right side-bar. This lets the AI bridge run in the background without leaving your current workspace.

![bridge-side-panel](https://www.epochgram.com/images/1.7.0-bridge-side-panel.png)

## Other improvements

- Reduced typing-time stutter by deferring heavy reindex and refresh work.
- Reset now excludes separate Marks and Pinned actions to avoid accidental note-content updates.
- Search suggestions now prioritize recently opened notes in the top results.
- Improved AI bridge stability.
- Small settings wording and UI consistency fixes.
